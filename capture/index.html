Method

Capture is the drop zone for raw material from any model, voice note, or half-baked thread. Don’t tidy it. Filing is the job of the desk.

  1. Paste or dump the raw text.
  2. Optional one-line: where it came from.
  3. You do not need to say “please save this.” Capture is the job.

What happens next

  • Durable facts → memory (privacy floor still applies).
  • Finished passes → Analyst pages + Drive when marked not-preliminary.
  • Needs Crystal yes (merge, public post, spend, outreach, live www/canon, durable delete) → one-line ask, then wait.

Sweep cadence: weekdays 10:00 Australia/Sydney.

Privacy floor

No family names, medical detail, street addresses, or secrets in durable public memory.
